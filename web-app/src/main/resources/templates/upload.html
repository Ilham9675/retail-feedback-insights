Upload customer reviews

Upload a .csv or .txt file with up to 200 reviews (max 1 MB). Gemini starts analysing right after the upload, about 25 reviews per request, and the dashboard shows the progress.

Error
CSV format

A header row is required. Only comment is mandatory; label (POSITIVE / NEUTRAL / NEGATIVE) enables the accuracy evaluation.

id,customer,department,date,comment,label
1,Jane Smith,Checkout,2025-05-01,"Fast checkout, friendly cashier.",POSITIVE
2,Ahmad,Grocery,2025-05-02,The milk was expired.,NEGATIVE

TXT format

Blocks separated by a blank line; the label in brackets is optional.

Feedback #1 [POSITIVE]

Department: Checkout
Date: 2025-05-01
